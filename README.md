# Blinkit Product Teardown 🚀

### Driving Customer Engagement, Retention & Sustainable Growth Through Data-Driven Product Innovation

A product analytics and strategy case study on **Blinkit**, India's quick-commerce platform, focused on improving repeat purchases, order frequency, fulfilment reliability, customer retention, and contribution margin.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

Quick commerce is becoming increasingly competitive, with platforms such as **Blinkit, Zepto, Swiggy Instamart and BigBasket** competing on speed, assortment, convenience and customer experience.

This project analyzes Blinkit's customer journey and identifies opportunities to move beyond speed as the primary differentiator.

### Core Decision Question

> **How might Blinkit increase repeat purchases and order frequency while improving fulfilment reliability and protecting contribution margin?**

The analysis identifies three major opportunity areas:

1. **Reliability**
2. **Replenishment**
3. **Household-level retention**

The overall strategic direction is:

**Speed → Reliability → Replenishment → Household Loyalty**

---

# 🎯 Objectives

The project aims to:

* Understand Blinkit's quick-commerce business model
* Analyze the customer journey from discovery to repeat purchase
* Identify major causes of low repeat purchases
* Identify gaps in fulfilment reliability
* Identify opportunities to create replenishment habits
* Improve customer retention and order frequency
* Develop product initiatives with measurable success metrics
* Prioritize initiatives using the **RICE framework**
* Create a 6-month product roadmap

---

# 🔍 Key Insights

### 1. Speed attracts, but reliability retains

Blinkit's ultra-fast delivery is a major acquisition and convenience advantage. However, a fast delivery is valuable only when the customer receives the **correct and complete basket**.

The project therefore identifies fulfilment reliability as an important driver of repeat purchases.

---

### 2. Low repeat purchase is driven by multiple gaps

The root-cause analysis identifies three major gaps:

* **Reliability Gap**

  * Product unavailable
  * Unwanted substitutions
  * Missing or damaged items
  * Refund/support friction

* **Replenishment Gap**

  * Manual reordering
  * No personalized refill timing
  * No one-tap recurring basket

* **Household Gap**

  * Fragmented family shopping
  * No shared shopping lists
  * Limited family benefits

---

### 3. Blinkit should move beyond speed

Competitive benchmarking suggests that speed is increasingly becoming a category standard.

The strategic opportunity is to position Blinkit around:

> **Speed → First Order**
> **Reliability → Repeat Purchase**
> **Intelligence → Higher Frequency**

---

### 4. Three product opportunities

The project proposes three major initiatives:

#### 🛒 Perfect Basket

Improve basket reliability through:

* Real-time availability
* Approved substitutes
* Instant refunds for errors
* Better product availability visibility
* Complete-basket suggestions

**Expected impact:** Increased trust and repeat purchases.

---

#### 🔄 Smart Refill

Create a personalized replenishment experience through:

* Recurring purchase detection
* Personalized refill reminders
* Predicted next refill windows
* One-tap reordering
* User-controlled reminders
* Alternative product recommendations

**Expected impact:** Higher order frequency and engagement.

---

#### 🏠 Household Benefits

Create a shared household shopping ecosystem through:

* Shared shopping lists
* Family member profiles and permissions
* Monthly essentials
* Shared reorder history
* Household bundles
* Personalized family benefits

**Expected impact:** Higher household retention and long-term loyalty.

---

# 📊 North Star & Success Metrics

The project proposes a hierarchy of metrics to measure sustainable growth.

### North Star Metric

**Successful recurring orders per active household**

> Complete and accurate recurring orders ÷ Active households

### Supporting Metrics

| Metric                           | Purpose                                   |
| -------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Contribution per Order           | Measures profitable growth                |
| First-to-Second Order Conversion | Measures conversion into repeat behaviour |
| Smart Refill Engagement Rate     | Measures replenishment adoption           |
| D90 Retention                    | Measures long-term retention              |
| Perfect-Order Rate               | Measures fulfilment reliability           |
| Promo Burn per Order             | Protects contribution margin              |

The project emphasizes that **speed acquires customers, reliability earns repeat orders, intelligence increases frequency, and guardrails protect profitable growth.**

# 🧪 Experimentation Strategy

### Perfect Basket Experiment

A/B test the Perfect Basket experience across selected dark stores.

**Control:**
Existing customer experience

**Pilot:**
Perfect Basket features + high-frequency categories

### Hypothesis

> Better basket accuracy will improve customer trust, repeat purchase behaviour and retention.

Success metrics include:

* Perfect-order rate
* Fill rate
* Substitution acceptance rate
* Refund rate
* Customer-support contacts
* Contribution per order

---

# 📈 RICE Prioritization

The proposed initiatives were prioritized using:

> **RICE Score = Reach × Impact × Confidence ÷ Effort**

| Priority | Initiative         | RICE Score |
| -------- | ------------------ | ---------: |
| 🥇 1     | Perfect Basket     |       48.0 |
| 🥈 2     | Smart Refill       |       37.5 |
| 🥉 3     | Household Benefits |       14.6 |

Therefore, **Perfect Basket** is recommended as the first initiative, followed by **Smart Refill** and then **Household Benefits**.

### Weeks 5–8

Pilot Perfect Basket.

### Weeks 9–16

Test Smart Refill with selected users.

### Weeks 17–24

Launch Household Benefits.

### After Week 24

Scale initiatives only if retention and contribution metrics improve.

# 🚀 Final Recommendation

The project recommends a phased strategy:

### 1️⃣ Fix Reliability

Launch **Perfect Basket** to improve order accuracy and customer trust.

### 2️⃣ Build Replenishment Habits
